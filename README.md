**bingevoid** /ˈbɪndʒ.vɔɪd/ — noun. *The hollow, restless quiet after finishing a series you loved, when no other show feels worth starting.*

A deterministic program scores candidate coinages on the brief's three criteria — a real gap in English, a definition that lands in one reading, and a word that is sayable, spellable and memorable — then prints the single best word and its one-line definition. The word and its definition are the whole entry.
